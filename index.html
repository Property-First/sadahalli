ABOUT THE PROJECT

Nambiar District 25 Phase 3

Nambiar District 25 on Sarjapur Road is one of Bangalore’s fastest-growing real estate destinations, offering excellent connectivity to major IT hubs and strong long-term appreciation potential. This premium 200+ acre integrated township features thoughtfully designed residences, modern infrastructure, expansive green spaces, and world-class amenities including a large clubhouse, sports facilities, and retail spaces, making it an ideal choice for both homebuyers and investors. Starting from ₹1.6 Cr*, the project combines luxury living with a future-ready community environment.
